About Price Check

What is Price Check?

Price Check is a daily guessing game for Counter-Strike 2 players. Each day brings five skins — you look at the item and guess what it's actually worth on the market. The closer your guess, the more points you score, up to 100 per round and 500 for the day.

How is it scored?

Scoring is based on how close your guess is as a ratio to the real price, not a flat dollar difference — so guessing $40 on a $50 skin scores the same as guessing $400 on a $500 one. Exact or near-exact guesses score close to 100; wildly off guesses score close to 0.

Where do the images and prices come from?

Item images are pulled from the public Counter-Strike 2 item schema, the same in-game icons you'd see on CSFloat and every other marketplace. Prices are a hand-curated snapshot of real recent market values rather than a second-by-second live feed, since a live marketplace price requires an authenticated API connection that can't safely run from a public browser page. Actual live prices move constantly and may differ from what's shown here.

Why can't I sign in with my CSFloat account?

Price Check doesn't connect to any trading account, and it never asks for one. It's a standalone guessing game, not a marketplace.
